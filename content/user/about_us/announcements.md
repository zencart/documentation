Subscribing to announcements is a great way to stay up to date on what's happening with Zen Cart.  You also get important security bulletins as soon as they are released.  

You may sign up for Release Announcements by going to the [forum home page](https://www.zen-cart.com/forums), scrolling down to "Zen Cart Release Announcements," and clicking the **Subscribe** button on the right.   Please note that even if you signed up before on our old vBulletin forum, you still need to do it again on the new forum.

[See past release announcements](https://www.zen-cart.com/forumdisplay.php?2-Zen-Cart-Release-Announcements).
