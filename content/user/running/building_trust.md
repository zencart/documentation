Some steps you can take to make your cart more trustworthy: 

- Creating an [about us](/user/storefront_pages/about_us/) page
- Displaying [customer reviews](/user/storefront_pages/reviews/)
- Publishing clear [shipping and return policies](/user/storefront_pages/shippinginfo/)
- Publishing clear [privacy policies](/user//storefront_pages/privacy/) 
- Making it easy to get support - fill in the "Telephone - Customer Service" and "Store Address and Phone" fields on the [Admin > Configuration > My Store](/user/admin_pages/configuration/configuration_mystore/) page, and build your [contact us](/user/storefront_pages/contact_us/) page.

See [this Google article](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13693865), which is about businesses using Google Merchant Center, but applies to any online business.
